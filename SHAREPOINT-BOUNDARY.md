# Microsoft 365 boundary specification

## Current access model

David A. Ochabski and the current Board of Directors should have access to all
internal QED-AI working and confidential areas. Board membership should be
represented by one Microsoft 365 or Entra group rather than repeated individual
permissions wherever the available license permits.

## Prepared future model

Future volunteers receive access only through separate volunteer workspaces or
libraries. They must not inherit access to Board, legal, tax, administrative,
security, or restricted-source areas merely by joining the tenant or a broad
organization group.

Recommended boundaries:

1. **Board and Official Records** — Board-only; authoritative governance record.
2. **Legal, Tax, and Administration** — Board-only.
3. **Research Evidence Vault** — Board-only initially; later explicit project
   groups may receive item- or library-scoped access.
4. **Programs and Publication Staging** — Board-only initially; selected future
   project spaces may permit volunteers.
5. **Volunteer Workspaces** — dormant until needed; no inherited access to the
   four areas above.

The existing QED-AI SharePoint document library currently exposes an inherited
tenant-wide principal. Confidential migration must wait until the Board-only
boundary is confirmed and tested with a non-Board account.
